Built for the workout

Everything you need. Nothing in the way.

Create structured routines or start immediately with Quick Workout. JAK keeps logging fast while still giving you the tools needed for detailed training.

01

Flexible workouts

Create workout templates, organize exercises and supersets, or begin an empty Quick Workout and build it as you train.

02

Fast workout logging

Record sets, weights, repetitions and timed exercises with a workflow designed to keep you focused.

03

Timers and rest periods

Use exercise timers, rest timers, countdown sounds and notification controls throughout your workout.

04

History and progress

Review completed workouts, previous values and training goals directly inside the app.

05

Private by design

No JAK account is required. Workout information is stored locally on your device by default.

06

Optional backup

Back up your information to your own private Google Drive application-data folder when you choose to enable it.

Privacy first

Your workout data belongs to you.

JAK has no developer-operated backend and does not include advertising or behavioral analytics. Google Drive backup is optional and can be disconnected whenever you choose.
